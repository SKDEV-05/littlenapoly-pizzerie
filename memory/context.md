## 3. Culinary Ground Truth & Certified Ingredients
- **Pizza Crust:** Neapolitan 48-hour slow cold fermentation with Caputo 00 flour, wood-fired at 485°C in 60–90 seconds producing prominent charred leopard spots (*cornicione maculato*).
- **Tomato Sauce Base:** Certified San Marzano D.O.P. dell'Agro Sarnese-Nocerino.
- **Yellow Sauce Base ("Pizze Gialle"):** Sweet Campania Yellow Datterino (*Datterino Giallo*).
- **Cheese:** Mozzarella di Bufala Campana D.O.P., Sorrento Fior di Latte, Provola Affumicata.
- **Charcuterie:** 24-month aged Prosciutto San Daniele D.O.P., Salame Napoli Piccante, Salsiccia Napoletana Fresca.
